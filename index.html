What you'll do

🏯 Live in a hidden village

Pick Hidden Pine, Hidden Ember or Hidden Tide. Walk the streets with other players in real time, chat, and visit the Academy, Mission Hall, Tool Shop, Library, Hospital and Auction House.

Your village is your team for life: earn points for it, join its wars over territory, and rise to become its Kage.

⚔️ Tactical hex battles

Every fight is turn-based on a hex grid. Spend your action points to move, take cover behind rocks, and chain ninjutsu, genjutsu, taijutsu and weapon techniques.

Pick your loadout before each battle. Five elements counter each other, stronger jutsu have cooldowns, and counter scrolls let you dodge, cleanse or dispel at the perfect moment.

🗺️ Quests, bosses and rare loot

Leave the village gate to explore the Land of Five Winds: 21 places, story quest lines, and boss lairs.

Team up with two friends to take down bosses like the Mist Reaper and the Scorpion King, and hunt for rare weapons and armour to trade at the Auction House.

🥷 Make your own ninja

Choose your hair, skin, outfit and headband. You're born with one of five elements. A lucky few awaken a rare bloodline at the Genin exam.

Climb the ranks from Academy student to Genin, Chunin and Jonin, all the way to level 60. A maxed ninja is a real achievement.

📱 Play anywhere

Shinobi Realms runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet and phone. There's nothing to install. Your ninja is saved to your Google account, so you can train on your phone and battle on your computer.

On a phone, use Add to Home Screen to open it like an app.

Questions

Is Shinobi Realms free?

Yes. Shinobi Realms is free to play in your web browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs in your browser on desktop and on phones. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How do I sign in?

Sign in with your Google account. Your ninja is saved to it, so you can carry on from any device.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Players in the same village see each other live and share a chat. You can duel other players, team up in groups of three against bosses, and fight together in village wars.

How long does it take to reach max level?

Reaching level 60 as a Jonin takes a dedicated player around three months. Training every 15 minutes and a short daily session of missions is the fastest path.

Where can I find other players?

Join our Discord server: pick your village, find a team for boss raids, report bugs and tell us what to build next. Raid night is every Friday at 8pm UK time.

Is it based on an existing show?

Shinobi Realms is inspired by classic ninja anime, but its world, villages and characters are all original.
