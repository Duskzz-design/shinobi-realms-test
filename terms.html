Shinobi Realms Terms of Use

Shinobi Realms is provided free and as-is, in early access. Features and saved progress may change while the game is being built.

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate speech or offensive names, in chat or anywhere else in the game. We may remove messages, names or pictures that break these rules.

Fair play

  • One account per person. Don't use second accounts to help your main ninja, for example to win Arena duels on purpose, move ryo or items through the Auction House, or gain village or war points.
  • No bots or scripts. Play the game yourself. Don't use programs that train, fight or click for you.
  • No cheating or hacking. Don't change the game, interfere with our servers, or try to get rewards you didn't earn.
  • Report bugs, don't use them. If you find a bug that gives an unfair advantage, tell us at support@shinobirealms.com instead of using it.
  • Don't share, sell or buy accounts, or trade game items or ryo for real money.

Use 🚩 Report player on any ninja's profile to tell us about cheating or bad behaviour. Reports are private.

To keep the game fair, we look at how the game is played (for example, how long and at what times players are active, and how fast they progress). If we find that someone broke these rules, we may reset their progress, remove rewards, or suspend or close their account.

Shinobi Realms is an original game and is not affiliated with any existing anime, manga or game.

Questions or problems: support@shinobirealms.com.
