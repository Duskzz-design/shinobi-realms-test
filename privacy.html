Shinobi Realms Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Shinobi Realms is a free browser game. This page explains what we keep about you, why, and how to delete it.

What we receive when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link with our login provider (Supabase). We never see your Google password, and we cannot read your Gmail, contacts or files. We use these details only to sign you in and keep your ninja saved. They are never shown to other players.

When you sign in with Discord, Discord shares your Discord user ID, username, email address and avatar link with our login provider (Supabase). We never see your Discord password, and we cannot read your messages, servers or friends. We use these details only to sign you in and keep your ninja saved.

If you create an account with your email address, we store that address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never see or store your actual password. We use your email only to confirm your account, to send password-reset links you ask for and, if you tick the box, game news. These emails are sent for us by Brevo.

Game news emails

We only send game news (updates, events, new seasons) if you tick "Send me game news" when you sign up. Every news email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us at support@shinobirealms.com to stop at any time.

What we store about your ninja

Your ninja's name, look, village, level, rank, stats, items, badges and in-game progress. Other players can see your ninja's profile (name, picture, look, village, rank, level, title, element, stats, equipment, badges and records such as missions won) and where your ninja is standing in the village.

Profile pictures

You can upload a picture for your profile. It is public: every player can see it. Please use art or a drawing, not a photo of yourself or anyone else. Players can report pictures; a picture reported by several players is hidden, and repeated reports turn uploads off for that account. You can remove your picture at any time from your profile, and it is deleted with your account.

Chat

Chat messages are shown to players in your village (village chat) or to everyone (world chat). We keep a copy of each message for up to 30 days so we can deal with reports of abuse, then it is deleted automatically. Messages are checked against a word filter, and players can report messages to us.

On your device

The game stores your login session and a few settings (such as players you have muted) in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who processes your data

Your data is stored by our database and login provider, Supabase. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Account emails are sent by Brevo, and Cloudflare Turnstile runs a quick check on the sign-in screen to stop bots creating accounts. Like any website, these providers briefly log technical information such as IP addresses to keep their services secure. We do not sell your data or show third-party ads.

Deleting your account

Open the in-game ☰ Menu and choose Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your ninja, all your game records, your chat messages and your login. If you cannot sign in any more, email support@shinobirealms.com and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@shinobirealms.com to make a request. We will reply within one month, as the law requires.

Contact us

Questions about your data or your account: support@shinobirealms.com. You can also find us on our Discord server.
